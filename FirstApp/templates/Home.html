How SUTD Works

A simple, transparent, and direct process to share and care for our regional community.

📝

1. Register & List

Register as a contributor and easily list your unused household items (clothes, books, electronics) online.

🚚

2. Quality Check & Collect

Our dedicated team performs doorstep collection, checks item condition, and updates stock inventory logs.

🎁

3. Direct Redistribution

We match collected stocks with verified regional beneficiaries, shelter homes, and partner NGOs transparently.
