About Our Project

SUTD (Share of Unused Things at Doorstep) is a digital platform that connects people who have old or unused items with those who need them. We aim to reduce waste, promote reuse, and ensure that generosity reaches the right doorstep.

How It Works

1. Register

Create your account as a donor or volunteer.

2. List Items

Add details and photos of items you want to donate.

3. Verify & Approve

Our team verifies and matches items with needy people.

4. Deliver

Items are delivered to the right doorstep.
